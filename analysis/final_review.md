## Findings requiring clear final-report wording

1. **The modeling endpoint is a historical export label, not validated homogeneous CDK2 biochemistry.** Curation calls 122 rows eligible for explicitly declared CHEMBL5736732 exact IC50 export analysis, while preserving unresolved row-level cyclin/ATP context. This meets the declared-endpoint contract, but the final report must retain that qualification near every performance claim. Source agreement verifies transcription, not assay comparability. Recommended action: call the evaluation an exploratory audit of historical export labels, and do not describe these 122 rows as a validated CDK2 training set. Current curation reasons and model plan already carry the necessary qualification.

2. **Do not imply that the change from original R² 0.960 to descriptor R² -0.603 isolates one defect.** The original uses 262 rows and random-row partitioning; the audited models use 122 curated rows with scaffold/collision groups and chemical weights. Both curation and evaluation protocol differ. The common-fold comparison of retrospective-index RF (R² 0.984) with descriptor RF is the cleaner feature-provenance diagnostic. The supplied vendor README independently establishes the index was created after assays. Recommended action: explain that the same-fold leaked-feature model remaining excellent demonstrates why grouping alone cannot repair target-derived features; avoid attributing the entire original-to-curated score change solely to splitting.

3. **Contradictory repeated chemical labels are preserved, not scientifically resolved.** Five full graphs have distinct source activities with unequal labels, including a 1.538-log span. Grouping and total chemical weight 1 prevent their easy multiplication into independent test units. They do not justify interpreting the within-chemical labels as experimental replicates, deriving assay precision, or treating the nearest-neighbor mean as experimentally validated potency. Recommended action: retain the reported ambiguity and lack of interval calibration. The current code/plan appropriately do so. Omitting the planned shuffle is disclosed; no permutation evidence should be claimed.

4. **Prospective gate still has limited precision.** The allocated assays give two newly prepared ENGAGE experiments only for CED-08. Existing TE observations plus new ENGAGE supply useful confirmation for several others, but assay independence and historical replication are unknown; CED-03/23 remain single-new-ENGAGE experiments. Half-life values of 22 minutes are near the >20-minute preference and are not repeated. Recommended action: qualify a passing compound as supporting a focused next cycle, with uncertainty unresolved, rather than declare robust development readiness. Require interpretable matched biochemical curves and check soluble assay exposure before computing/using a tenfold ratio.
