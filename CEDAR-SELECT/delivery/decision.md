# CEDAR: conditionally switch the discovery emphasis; earn synthesis through a cellular bridge

**Recommend a conditional switch to the public spiro-lactam direction, with two exploratory designs and two historical diagnostic comparators. Do not authorize a six-compound optimization batch or claim a selective lead yet.** The next tranche should establish whether this direction has a reproducible cellular CDK2/CDK1 window. Its matched public biochemical evidence is a better starting point than the incumbent inventory's unconfirmed translation, but superiority in cells remains unproved.

The incumbent CED-01–22 chemistry is largely benzimidazole/aminopyrimidine based; CED-23/24 are separate purine diagnostics. The predecessor's eight-compound cycle was a proposal, not completed biology. Its simulated engagement, solubility and stability remain scenario context. We preserve that work but do not count its proposed results or vendor model as evidence for new synthesis.

The expanded packet supplies 60 compounds from one public spiro study, 45 exact biochemical pairs and 15 bounded CDK1 results. In its defined 1 mM ATP CDK2/E1 versus CDK1/B1 context, 47/60 meet the ≤100 nM and ≥10-fold preferences, including bound-supported passes. This is an enriched, nonrandom study subset, not a discovery success rate or evidence of cellular safety. The eight coverage analogues duplicate 16 of these labels.

An independent purine alternative deserves preservation: Coxon compound73 reports 44±12 nM CDK2 and 86,000±4,000 nM CDK1 (about1,955-fold) at12.5µM ATP, largely through loss of CDK1 inhibition relative to its phenyl parent. It showed no/limited growth effects up to30µM in five tested lines, without established intracellular exposure or target engagement. Its enzyme ratio is compelling; its cellular window is unresolved. We favor the available spiro comparator for the immediate bridge, not because its smaller ratio is superior. Reopen the purine branch if qualified engagement/exposure rescues73 or the spiro branch fails. [Structural and biological comparison](../structure-work/report.md).

Three completed computational/research rounds changed the action:

1. **Curate and compare.** Historical examples 39 and 41 give CDK2/CDK1 IC50 of 7/1200 and 5/730 nM: ratios 171 and 146. Example56 gives 10/800 nM, ratio80. More fluorination does not consistently improve discrimination. The 7→5 nM difference has no supplied replicate uncertainty and does not justify chasing potency alone.
2. **Challenge prediction and structure.** Training-selected 3-neighbor potency prediction had development MAE0.299 log units versus0.242 for the mean baseline. Selectivity selected the mean; no tested model met the frozen joint selection gate. Deposited purine complexes support a protein-state hypothesis, but ligand geometry limitations and different ligand/cyclin/crystal contexts preclude transferring an energetic explanation to the spiro designs. These results remove model score and an asserted structural selectivity mechanism from the synthesis justification.
3. **Choose discriminating chemistry and biology.** Public cellular studies of independent selective CDK2 inhibitors support measuring target engagement, biomarker context and functional response together. They do not supply labels for our designs. A proposed hydroxyethyl compound was rejected as new after finding it already in the packet. The remaining pair tests added distal volume versus terminal polarity, with no assumed potency improvement.

The cellular thresholds are project decision rules, not validated safety margins. **Reverse the switch** if authentic C39/C41 fail the matched biochemical preferences or fail the cellular gate despite adequate exposure and a working benchmark. Drop D02 if added carbon loses the biochemical preference without an exposure advantage. Drop D01 if added polarity worsens cellular translation without improving soluble exposure. If every tested direction lacks normal-cell separation, stop advancement rather than buy another potency series. A convincing incumbent matched cellular result would also reopen the allocation. No new measurements, clinical benefit, unseen-study prediction or completed synthesis is claimed.
