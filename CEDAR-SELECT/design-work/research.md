## Evidence that changes the next experiment

The supplied attributed exact observations (coverage/analogue_evidence.json) give Example39 CDK2/CDK1 IC50 7/1200 nM, Example41 5/730 nM, and Example56 10/800 nM. Conditional CDK1/CDK2 ratios are respectively 171,146,80. Increasing terminal fluorination is not a monotonic improvement in discrimination. Example39→41 improves CDK2 pIC50 by 0.146 but improves CDK1 pIC50 by 0.216, decreasing log discrimination by 0.070. Example41→56 decreases CDK2 pIC50 by 0.301 and CDK1 pIC50 by 0.040; discrimination falls 0.261. These are two correlated edges among three chemicals, no independent replication or uncertainty estimates. Example69/70 give 6/800 and 6/820 nM: side-chain stereochemical resolution alone lacks demonstrated biochemical benefit in that pair. These local results argue against selecting by maximum fluorination or claiming meaningful distinctions among rounded low-nanomolar values. External primary biology provides a stronger cellular test design than another biochemical rank. [House et al., Cancer Research 2025, PMID39945650](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39945650/) reports BLU-222 profiling with 1mM-ATP biochemical assays, NanoBRET, OVCAR-3 pRb Thr821/826 and pLamin S22 readouts, plus CCNE1/Rb/p16 response analysis. Those readouts were acquired at different treatment times and cannot by themselves be divided into a universally comparable cellular ratio. [House et al., npj Breast Cancer 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41523-025-00851-7) tested several CDK2 inhibitors, found a composite biomarker more informative than CCNE1 alone, and supports including Rb/p16 context and an orthogonal CDK2 chemotype. [Dietrich et al., Cancer Discovery 2024, PMID38047585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38047585/) provides INX-315 cell-cycle/senescence and tumor-model evidence. These reports support benchmark controls; their potency values are not merged into the fitted spiro assay. Neither clinical status nor human safety is inferred. There is insufficient verified route/identity work here to recommend de novo synthesis of BLU-222 or INX-315 within this tranche.

## Falsifiable tranche gates

1. Re-measure C39/C41 in paired CDK2/E1 and CDK1/B1 assays at 1mM ATP, with source-matched conditions as closely as possible; qualify curves and interference. Include CDK2/A2 to reveal cyclin dependence and an orthogonal activity readout if TR-FRET interference is suspected. D02 is first synthesize-if-feasible; D01 waits for an informative solubility/permeability bottleneck or available parallel chemistry at no delay to the core tests.
2. Establish matched-time live-cell CDK2 and CDK1 engagement if assay technology is feasible, alongside cellular exposure, solubility and microsomal stability. Use pRb/EdU and pLamin/cell-cycle morphology as supporting functional readouts, not substitutes for kinase-specific engagement. Test OVCAR-3 plus a verified Rb-impaired or CCNE1-normal model and a proliferating nontransformed comparator. Obtain cell authentication and current biomarker checks. An orthogonal benchmark chemotype (BLU-222 or INX-315, identity verified before procurement recommendation) separates a failed molecule from a failed pathway assay.
3. Programme-defined advancement: biochemical CDK2 ≤100nM and ratio ≥10 are necessary preferences. Proposed cellular gate: CDK2 engagement <150nM and CDK1 engagement at least10-fold higher at matched time/exposure, functional suppression in the biomarker-positive model, and ≥3-fold separation from nontransformed growth inhibition. These are decision thresholds, not validated safety margins. Use ≥3 independent biological days with concentration-response intervals; if intervals fail to separate compounds, carry the simpler route/less exposure-liability compound rather than name a potency winner. If no credible matched engagement assay is available, report the window unresolved, not passed.

Reverse the switch if authentic C39/C41 fail paired biochemical qualification or fail the cellular engagement/functional gate despite confirmed intracellular exposure and working benchmark. Drop D02 if the extra carbon loses biochemical preference without an exposure benefit; drop D01 if added polarity fails to improve soluble exposure or causes an unacceptable biochemical-to-cellular shift. A common inability to obtain normal-cell separation stops this tranche's advancement, even if biochemical ratios remain attractive. Additional designs beyond this pair would not presently buy enough independent information.
