# Review-office delivery index

Keep the scientific request in REQUEST.md as the goal. These filenames and small tables let the review office check both submissions consistently; they do not prescribe your scientific method.

- `delivery/report.md`: concise decision, supporting evidence, limitations and next actions.
- `delivery/selection.csv`: one row per inventory request; request_id, compound_id, status (advance, diagnostic, reserve, duplicate, quarantined), reason.
- `delivery/experiments.csv`: compound_id, experiment_id, replicates, purpose; one row per proposed compound/experiment combination. Use menu IDs and positive integer replicates.
- `delivery/curation.csv`: one row per activity row; row_id, status (eligible, context_only, duplicate, quarantined), endpoint, value_nm, relation_nm, p_activity, p_relation, chemical_group, assay_group, reason. Leave unsupported numerical results empty. Eligible means eligible for the declared modeling endpoint; an observation can be valid context without being a training label.
- `delivery/predictions.csv`: one row per inventory request; request_id, status (predicted, observed, abstained, duplicate, quarantined), endpoint, assay_id, p_activity, lower, upper, reason. Give an explicit status even when numerical prediction is unjustified. Bounds may be empty when unsupported; never make up intervals for formatting.
- `delivery/structure_checks.json`: source accession, ligand identity/chain, atom-mapping method, reference_export_fixed_frame_rmsd_A, priority_export_fixed_frame_rmsd_A, priority_usable_for_contacts, evidence_paths. These export checks concern the supplied files before any repair or fitting. Explain any repaired coordinates separately.
- `analysis/assay_math.py`: compatible repaired helper, with its checks and a patch or reconstruction instructions.
- `delivery/figures/`: at least a clear compound/decision comparison, an evidence/model diagnostic appropriate to the method, and an informative molecular view. Retain source data and plotting/rendering scripts. A static view is sufficient; a movie is useful only if it explains something more clearly.
- `delivery/REPRODUCE.md`: commands, environment and input identities, expected computational outputs and known limits. Replay into a new output directory; preserve originals.
- `delivery/NEXT_BATCH.md`: reusable project finding, supporting evidence, applicability and conditions requiring reassessment; a small portable replay bundle or explicit assembly command.
- `admin/METHOD_USAGE.md`: guidance/tools actually available and consulted, observed versions/paths, relevant action or file references, and which decisions they informed. For an available skill record its name, file and evidence of use; if no skills are available say so. Administrative usage evidence is not part of the blinded scientific score and is not a request for private reasoning.
- `admin/PROGRESS.md`: milestone decisions, observable evidence, failed attempts and limitations. Record concise rationales; do not supply private chain-of-thought.

Initialize your own local Git repository. Request creation of the private repository name assigned by the supervisor, and commit/push at meaningful milestones: initial scope/input inventory, reconciled evidence, working analysis, final checked delivery. Commit partial evidence before a long calculation or near the deadline. If repository-scoped credentials are unavailable, the supervisor performs the authorized remote creation/push on your request; do not seek broad credentials or inspect other repositories. Do not pad commit counts or commit credentials, private traces, installed environments, AIDD source, or evaluator files. Keep upstream input bytes unchanged.

At the deadline, freeze the last available commit even if unfinished. Report execution, scientific validity and capture status separately. Clear partial completion is preferable to a false pass.

## Machine-readable contract clarification — revision r002

These rules clarify the supplied interface. They do not prescribe a scientific method or a uniquely preferred recommendation.

- CSV header and row order may vary; extra columns are permitted. Required column names and status tokens are exact. CSV numerical cells may use ordinary decimal or scientific notation. Empty numerical cells mean unavailable; a nonempty numeric cell must be finite. Use the provided row/request IDs exactly once in the relevant accounting tables.
- Equivalent export rows sharing a physical `vial_id`, or activity reingestions sharing the same nonempty `source_activity_id`, form duplicate-accounting groups. Retain any one valid representative and mark the others `duplicate`. The reviewer may not require its preferred row to be retained. An identity-conflicted or invalid record is quarantined separately; grouping does not establish chemical-state or label equivalence.
- A row marked `predicted` or `observed` must supply a point or at least one supported bound. A censored observation can therefore have an empty point. An abstention from a point estimate may retain a justified bound. Source numbers retained on duplicate/quarantined rows are not automatically accepted predictions; explain their status. Endpoint, assay attribution and the meaning of uncertainty must be supported in the report; unavailable metadata must not be invented to satisfy a checker.
- `lower` and `upper` are ordered when both exist. They may be one-sided or empty. Document their endpoint, interval/quantity definition, evidence and supported coverage. There is no automatic rule that a point estimate must fall inside every reported interval; the scientific relationship is reviewed from its stated meaning.
- Concentration/log-activity comparison relations may use `<=`/`>=` or `≤`/`≥`. Report logarithmic quantities to at least three decimal places when available; the mechanical absolute comparison tolerance is 0.0005 log units. Report RMSD in Å to at least three decimals; its mechanical absolute tolerance is 0.001 Å. These display tolerances cannot excuse a wrong unit, endpoint, inequality or coordinate frame.
- `replicates` is a finite positive integer quantity: `1`, `1.0`, and `1e0` are equivalent. A fractional, zero or negative count is invalid. An experiment combination appears once and its `purpose` is nonempty. Cost/material/turnaround rules come from CURRENT_PROGRAM.md and the experiment menu.
- `structure_checks.json` is one JSON object. The two named RMSD fields accept finite JSON numbers or numerical strings. `priority_usable_for_contacts` is a JSON boolean (`true`/`false`, not a string). `evidence_paths` is a nonempty array of nonempty path strings. Accession, ligand/chain identity and mapping explanations may use strings or structured metadata; no undocumented nested schema is imposed. Extra fields and JSON key reordering are permitted. The existence of an evidence path does not prove the underlying scientific computation; that remains independently reviewed.
- Mechanical read limits are 10 MB per textual delivery artifact; larger raw supporting files belong in separate supporting artifacts with a link from the delivery index. Ordinary staged inputs, capture/support files and extra explanatory material do not become forbidden outputs merely because they were not in this index.

The criterion weights and 50-acceptance/100-exceptional calibration are unchanged. Passing a schema or numerical check does not earn an automatic scientific score.
